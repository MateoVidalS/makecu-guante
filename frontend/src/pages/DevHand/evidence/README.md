The grayscale capture deliberately separates the two poses: target hook with an
open actual hand. The translucent curled outline and solid extended fingers
remain distinguishable without hue.

`performance.json` records the opt-in steady-state browser measurement with
synthetic camera capture and a 30 Hz mutable stub. This does not establish
performance with a physical camera or MediaPipe inference; repeat the gallery
check on the demo laptop during integration.
