Robotic glove assembly

An 80.5 × 80 × 47 mm printed module sits under the wrist and forearm, held by two straps. It is rigid and spans the wrist, so it keeps it straight. Inside, the five spools sit at the same height, just under the lid. Each thread leaves its spool horizontally and tangentially over the front, rises about 10° to the palm and runs close to the skin through guides sewn to the glove up to the fingertip. No sleeves or tubes.

Parts

Table setup

  1. Put on the glove with the module under the wrist. Close the front strap around the hand and the back strap around the forearm.
  2. Palm down, with the front of the module at the table edge: the hand sticks out and the fingers close in the air.
  3. Forearm on a towel folded to about 4 cm, so it sits level with the module.
  4. Webcam in front, on the thumb side (at the corner of the table), 30–40 cm from the fingers and higher than the hand, pointing diagonally.
  5. Laptop in front of the patient, on the thumb side, with the screen facing them.
  6. Breadboard with the ESP32 and the INA219 on the table, behind the base; the STOP button on the other side.

Assembly order

  1. Screw in the 3 front-row servos and the 2 back-row ones, shaft up and at 0°.
  2. Screw each spool to its cross horn and mount it on the servo.
  3. Sew onto the glove, on the palm side: an anchor at each fingertip, one guide per phalanx and one on the palm at each knuckle.
  4. Route each thread: anchor, guides, the module's front exit and spool. In order from thumb to pinky; index and ring pass through the gap between the front spools. Knot it with the finger open and the thread just taut.
  5. Sew the back-of-hand elastics, from each fingertip to the glove cuff.
  6. Run the servo cables out through the back slot to the breadboard and close the lid with 4 screws.

Before printing: measure the actual servo (body, tabs, shaft height) and one finger's thread travel, and adjust the parameters in hardware/cad/piezas.py. The module is made for the right hand; for the left, mirror the module and lid in the slicer.
